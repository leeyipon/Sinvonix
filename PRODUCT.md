# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

B2B buyers evaluating a software/AI/marketing/design partner — founders,
product leads, and execs at growth-stage companies — usually mid-research,
comparing agencies/studios before a discovery call. They scan fast for proof
of competence (real stack, real numbers, real process) over generic agency
marketing language.

## Product Purpose

Sinvonix (brand name "Nimbus") is a digital studio's marketing site. It
exists to win discovery calls: explain the four service lines (software
development, AI solutions, digital marketing, UI/UX design), prove
credibility with concrete outcomes/stats, and convert visitors into leads via
contact/booking flows. Success = qualified inbound leads who already trust
the studio's competence before the call.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, technical-but-clear. Copy leans on real numbers and
plain-English specifics ("cut page load from 3.1s to 0.4s") over vague claims
("industry-leading"). Visually: clean SaaS-adjacent product-marketing
aesthetic — deep blue primary, teal-tinted neutrals, Space Grotesk display +
Inter body — engineered rather than decorative.

## Anti-references

Not generic cream/gradient-hero AI-agency slop; not overwrought enterprise
consultancy stiffness. Avoid stock-photo warmth and hero-metric-card clichés
beyond what's already committed in the shipped design.

## Design Principles

- Prove, don't claim — real numbers and specifics beat adjectives.
- Every section should read as evidence of the studio's own craft (the site
  is itself a work sample).
- Motion and detail should feel engineered, not decorative — intentional,
  restrained, purposeful.
- Respect the existing token system (DESIGN.md) — extend, don't fork it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA is an explicit sales point (stated in the UI/UX Design service FAQ:
"colour contrast, keyboard flows, focus states and reduced-motion support are
part of the work, not an add-on") — hold the site itself to that bar.
